My Letter To You 💗

My dearest Priscila,

Happy first anniversary, babe. ❤️

I still think about how we met on HelloTalk on 7 August 2026. At first, we barely talked — maybe once a day. And then somehow, without either of us planning it, something changed. We started liking each other, and suddenly our messages and video calls became part of our whole day and night.

In such a short time, you became someone I look forward to. Someone I want to tell things to. Someone I want to laugh with, annoy a little, and dream with.

I love your kind soul. I love your patience. I love your smile. And honestly, I love your silly behavior too — even the little things you probably don't think are special.

Some of my favourite moments are when we talk about Jesus and when we talk about our future. Those conversations make what we have feel deeper than just words on a screen.

I've always wanted a wife like you — someone with a beautiful heart, someone I can grow with, pray with, laugh with, and build a future with. I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like, but I know I want to keep discovering it with you.

Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life, Priscila. This may only be our first month, but I hope it is only the beginning of a much, much longer story.

If I had to choose you again, knowing everything I know now…

I would still choose you. Every single time. ❤️
Forever your Saurabh,
